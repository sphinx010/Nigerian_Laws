        FACTUAL SUBMISSIONS OF WHAT OCCURRED (FROM THE CLIENT'S PERSPECTIVE)

        The following is a chronological sequence of material facts establishing the defense's case theory: that the Claimants are the aggressors, have consistently resorted to self-help and violence, and that the Defendants' actions have been lawful, administrative, and aimed at preserving the family estate.

        1. **Background and Administration of the Estate:** The property in dispute, situated at 29, Suru Street, off Akilo Road, Agege, Lagos State, is the estate of the late Chief Sikiru Raji Olomowewe. The estate was historically under the administration of a caretaker who collected rent and remitted it to a family account overseen by the uncle of the 1st and 2nd Defendants and 1st Claimant. The rent was distributed among the surviving wives and their children. The current head of the Olomowewe family is Simatu Ainla (also known as Simatu Raji), the oldest surviving sibling of the late Chief Sikiru.

        2. **The 1st Defendant's Interventions (June 2020):** Recognizing the dilapidation of the estate, the 1st Defendant (Mr. Akeem Raji), the eldest surviving son, with the general approval of the family, took on a maintenance project. He purchased windows, doors, and fittings to improve the estate but faced continuous frustration from the Claimants.

        3. **The 1st Claimant's Financial Misconduct (2021–2023):** Between 2021 and 2023, the 1st Claimant deceitfully instructed the 14 tenants to stop paying rent into the collective family account and instead directed payments into his personal account. Falsely claiming sole authority, the 1st Claimant amassed a total sum of ₦1,980,000.00 (One Million, Nine Hundred and Eighty Thousand Naira Only) for his personal use, without the knowledge or consent of the 1st Defendant or other beneficiaries.

        4. **Appointment of the 3rd and 4th Defendants:** To secure and properly develop the estate, the 1st Defendant, acting on behalf of the family, engaged the professional services of the 3rd Defendant (Barr. Olufemi Balogun, a legal practitioner) via a validly executed Power of Attorney. The Power of Attorney was attested to by the children of the deceased under the Idi-Igi system. The 3rd Defendant subsequently engaged the 4th Defendant (Engineer Yusuf Adewale) to manage the structural renovation.

        5. **Family Meeting and Notice to Quit:** A family meeting was convened with the Claimants and all tenants present, disclosing the renovation plans which necessitated a temporary evacuation of the occupants in compliance with Lagos State Town Planning Laws. No objections were raised. Following this, the 3rd Defendant served quit notices to the tenants. During this process, the Claimants harassed the 1st Defendant and confiscated the notices from the tenants.

        6. **The Egbeda Meeting:** The 1st Claimant falsely reported to their uncle, Chief Rasheed Raji Olomowewe at Egbeda, that the 1st Defendant was forcefully evicting tenants. A meeting was convened where the 1st Claimant resorted to theatrical behavior and crying to secure empathy and conceal his embezzlement of the N5,040,000.00 rent. 

        7. **Violent Assault at the King's Meeting:** A subsequent family meeting was convened with the king at Egbeda to unify the family. During this meeting, after the Power of Attorney was reviewed and agreed upon without objection, the 1st Claimant unprovokedly and violently assaulted the 1st Defendant. The 1st Claimant battered the 1st Defendant, tearing his clothes to shreds and stripping him naked. The 1st Defendant was thrown into a gutter, sustaining deep bruises, lacerations, and permanent dental disfigurement, while the 2nd Claimant poured water on him. The 1st Defendant's phone and personal effects were destroyed. The king had to intervene to shield the 1st Defendant from further battery. 

        8. **Medical Attention:** Following the brutal assault, the 2nd Defendant had to assist the 1st Defendant, providing emergency medical first aid at a local chemist shop. The 2nd Defendant also had to purchase a new change of clothes for the 1st Defendant, who had been stripped completely naked.

        9. **Defacement of Property:** Unsatisfied with the physical violence, the 1st Claimant maliciously defaced the estate by inscribing defamatory statements publicly on the walls: "beware of 419 Akeem Raji & Jamiu Raji (Fraudsters), this house is note for sale!" This act was intended to injure the reputation of the 1st and 2nd Defendants.

        10. **Death Threats Against the 3rd and 4th Defendants:** When the 3rd Defendant and 4th Defendant commenced their lawful duties to survey and prepare the estate for renovation, the 1st Claimant issued severe death threats against them. The 1st Claimant threatened to kill both the lawyer and the engineer, explicitly threatening to pour acid on them and cause gruesome physical disfigurement. The 4th Defendant filed a formal statement at the Area F Police Station, Ikeja, regarding these threats.

        11. **Use of Thugs and Hoodlums:** The Defendants arranged for contractors to deliver building materials to the estate. The 1st Claimant mobilized street thugs, hoodlums, and Oodua Peoples Congress (OPC) / Omo Onile boys to harass the contractors and violently prevent the delivery. The contractors were surrounded and had to flee into their vehicles.

        12. **Arrest by the Police Task Force:** The 3rd Defendant called the Police Task Force to intervene. While the thugs fled, the 1st Claimant, his wife, and his son remained. They verbally insulted, resisted, and disrespected the police officers. Consequently, they were arrested for public nuisance, breach of peace, and gross insubordination. They were later released on bail by their in-laws with a warning.

        13. **Conclusion:** Following their release, a resolution meeting was convened by Chief Rasheed Raji Olomowewe. To preserve family peace, the 1st and 2nd Defendants elected not to pursue immediate legal action for the assault. However, the Claimants' current suit is a continuation of their bad faith. The 1st Claimant is merely an occupier who has not contributed to the estate's upkeep, and the 2nd Claimant has similarly contributed nothing. The material facts demonstrate conclusively that the Claimants are the violent aggressors attempting to usurp family property, while the Defendants have acted lawfully to protect and manage the estate.
