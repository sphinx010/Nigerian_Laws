STATEMENT OF DEFENCE

SAVE AND EXCEPT as is expressly admitted herein, the Defendants deny each and every allegation of fact contained in the Statement of Claim as if same were set out and traversed seriatim.

1. The Defendants admit paragraphs 2, 3, and 5 of the Statement of Claim.
2. The Defendants deny paragraphs 1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12 of the Statement of Claim and put the Claimants to the strictest proof thereof.
3. In answer to paragraph 1 of the Statement of Claim, the Defendants state that the Claimants do not represent the entire beneficiaries of the Estate of Late Chief Oladapo Adeyemo, as the Claimants procured the said Letters of Administration fraudulently and without the consent of the principal members of the family.
4. The Defendants shall contend at trial that this action is incompetent as constituted, the Claimants lacking the requisite authority to institute same in a representative capacity.
5. In response to paragraph 4 of the Statement of Claim, the Defendants deny that the property has been under the lawful management of the Claimants. The Defendants aver that the current head of the Adeyemo family is Madam Kikelomo Adeyemo, and the property has been managed by the family collectively.
6. In answer to paragraphs 6 and 8 of the Statement of Claim, the Defendants deny that the 3rd and 4th Defendants unlawfully invaded the property or commenced unauthorized demolition.
7. The Defendants aver that Madam Kikelomo Adeyemo, acting as the Family Head and with the consent of the principal members of the family, lawfully engaged the 3rd Defendant via a valid Power of Attorney to undertake the development and renovation of the property.
8. The Defendants further state that the 4th Defendant acted at all material times pursuant to the terms of his engagement by the 3rd Defendant for the said renovation works.
9. In response to paragraph 7 of the Statement of Claim, the Defendants state that lawful quit notices were issued to the tenants by the 3rd Defendant to facilitate the agreed renovation works in compliance with applicable laws.
10. In answer to paragraphs 9 and 10 of the Statement of Claim, the Defendants deny that the 1st Defendant directed tenants to pay rent into his personal account or diverted the sum of ₦8,500,000.00.
11. The Defendants aver that it was the 1st Claimant who, without the authority of the Family Head, diverted rental income from the tenants into his personal account between the years 2024 and 2025.
12. The Defendants further aver that the total sum diverted by the 1st Claimant amounts to ₦8,500,000.00 (Eight Million, Five Hundred Thousand Naira Only).
13. The Defendants aver that the Claimants instituted this action in bad faith to frustrate the lawful development of the property and conceal the 1st Claimant's diversion of estate funds.
14. The Defendants state that the Claimants’ claims are false, frivolous, and lacking in merit, and urge this Honourable Court to dismiss the suit in its entirety with substantial costs.
