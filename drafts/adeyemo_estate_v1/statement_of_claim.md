STATEMENT OF CLAIM

1. The Claimants are the Administrators of the Estate of Late Chief Oladapo Adeyemo, having been granted Letters of Administration in Suit No. ID/PRB/567/2025, and bring this action in that representative capacity.
2. The 1st and 2nd Defendants are siblings to the Claimants and beneficiaries of the Estate, while the 3rd Defendant is a legal practitioner and the 4th Defendant is an engineer engaged by the 1st and 2nd Defendants.
3. The property in dispute is a block of flats situated at No. 15, Adeyemo Crescent, Ikeja, Lagos State, within the jurisdiction of this Honourable Court.
4. The Claimants aver that the deceased died intestate on the 14th of March 2024, leaving behind the aforementioned property, which has been under the lawful management and administration of the Claimants.
5. The Claimants aver that the property was occupied by rent-paying tenants whose tenancies were valid and subsisting.
6. The Claimants aver that on the 10th of January 2026, the 1st and 2nd Defendants, without the consent or authority of the Claimants or the principal members of the family, forcefully invaded the property in the company of the 3rd and 4th Defendants.
7. The Claimants aver that the 3rd Defendant, purporting to act on a fraudulent Power of Attorney, issued unlawful quit notices to the lawful tenants of the property.
8. The Claimants aver that the 4th Defendant subsequently mobilized workmen to the site and commenced unauthorized demolition and renovation works, causing distress to the tenants and damage to the structure.
9. The Claimants aver that the 1st Defendant unilaterally directed the tenants to pay their annual rent directly into his personal bank account.
10. The Claimants aver that the 1st Defendant successfully diverted the total sum of ₦8,500,000 (Eight Million, Five Hundred Thousand Naira Only) belonging to the Estate.
11. The Claimants aver that despite repeated demands to halt the unlawful interference and remit the diverted funds, the Defendants have persisted in their actions.

**PARTICULARS OF SPECIAL DAMAGES**
i. Diverted rental income collected by the 1st Defendant: ₦8,500,000.00
**Total Special Damages:** ₦8,500,000.00

12. WHEREOF the Claimants claim against the Defendants jointly and severally as follows:
    a) A DECLARATION that the Claimants, as the lawful Administrators of the Estate, possess the exclusive right to manage and administer the property at No. 15, Adeyemo Crescent, Ikeja, Lagos State.
    b) AN ORDER of perpetual injunction restraining the Defendants, their agents, privies, or assigns from trespassing, renovating, or in any way interfering with the property.
    c) AN ORDER mandating the 1st Defendant to remit the sum of ₦8,500,000.00 (Eight Million, Five Hundred Thousand Naira Only) being special damages for diverted rental income belonging to the Estate.
    d) General damages in the sum of ₦10,000,000.00 (Ten Million Naira Only) for trespass and unlawful interference.
    e) Cost of this action.
